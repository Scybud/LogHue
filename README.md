# LogHue

**Everything you're working on. Nothing scattered.**

LogHue is a search-first workspace for tracking tasks, notes, and team progress without the noise of chasing updates across chats and tools.

It's built solo-first: your tasks and notes stand on their own from the moment you sign up, and workspaces layer collaboration on top when you need it, without ever making solo use feel like a stripped-down version of the real product.

---

## Overview

Most teams don't lack tools, they lack clarity.

Work gets scattered across chats, updates get missed, and progress becomes hard to follow. LogHue solves this by giving personal and team work a clear, structured home, and by making that structure fast to reach through search rather than buried in menus.

**LogHue solves this by structuring how work flows:**
- Personal tasks and notes exist independently, usable on day one with no team required
- Work can be duplicated into a workspace to share, without ever leaving the personal list
- Tasks are clearly assigned and tracked through to completion
- Progress is logged as it happens, separate from marking something actually done
- Everything, workspaces, tasks, and notes, is reachable instantly through search

## Core Features

### 🔍 Search-First Dashboard
The dashboard opens on search, not a wall of cards. A single input finds workspaces, tasks, and notes across the entire app, and understands shortcuts like "all tasks" or "all notes" to surface everything of that type at once.

### ⌨️ Command Palette
Press Ctrl+K (or Cmd+K) from anywhere to search, jump to a specific item, or run a command without leaving the keyboard. Typing `/[page name]` navigates directly to any page in the app.

### 🧩 Solo-First, Combined But Separate
Personal tasks and notes work fully standalone, no workspace required. When something's ready to share, it duplicates into a workspace without ever being removed from your personal list, so nothing you own gets locked behind team setup.

### 📌 Task and Progress Tracking
Tasks move between not-done and done, and can be unmarked, deleted, or duplicated into a workspace at any point. In a workspace, marking a task done is an admin or owner action, kept separate from a member logging their own progress on it.

### 📝 Notes, Built to Leave the App
Notes are written and stored on their own, then exported as TXT, Markdown, PDF, DOCX, or HTML when they need to go somewhere else. Any workspace member can view or download a shared note; only its creator can delete it.

### 💬 Workspace Discussions
Threaded discussions keep team conversation attached to the work itself instead of scattered across chat apps.

### 👥 Clear Roles
- **Owners** hold full control, including deleting the workspace or transferring ownership
- **Admins** manage members and review progress, without owner-level control
- **Members** create and work tasks, log progress, and participate in discussions

### 🔔 Deadline Reminders
Email and push notifications fire ahead of task deadlines, so nothing slips from being forgotten rather than from being unclear.

### 🌙 Calm and Focused Design
A clean, dark interface built to keep attention on the work, not the tool.

## How It Works

1. Start solo, tasks and notes work immediately, no workspace required
2. Create a workspace when you're ready to collaborate
3. Invite your team and assign tasks
4. Log progress as work happens
5. Review, discuss, and guide from within the workspace
6. Close tasks with a clear, shared record of what happened

## Who It's For

- **Individuals** who want structured personal tasks and notes, no team involved
- **Growing teams** that need shared structure without extra process
- **Managers** who want visibility without micromanaging
- **Small groups** tired of work getting lost across scattered chats and tools

---

## Example Workflow

Someone logs personal tasks and notes on their own, no workspace involved.

A project comes up, so they create a workspace and duplicate the relevant tasks into it, keeping the originals in their personal list.

A teammate logs progress on an assigned task. The workspace owner reviews it and marks the task done.

The note gets exported as a PDF to share outside LogHue. Nothing is lost, and nothing had to be rebuilt from scratch to make it shareable.
