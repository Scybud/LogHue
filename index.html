Why people choose LogHue

LogHue gives structure to your work without forcing everything into one place or leaving it scattered across a dozen apps.

Combined, not merged

Notes and tasks stay their own thing by default. Connect them to a workspace when it helps, keep them separate when it doesn't deleting one never touches the other.

Search that actually finds it

Built for one, ready for more

Calm and modern design

A focused interface that feels familiar and stays out of the way of real work.

What you can do with LogHue

Write notes freely

Create tasks on your own
