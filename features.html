Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

LogHue is built to keep your work organized without forcing it into one giant document or scattering it across a dozen apps. Write notes, track tasks, and connect them to a workspace only when it actually helps.

Core Features

Notes That Stand On Their Own

Write freely without deciding where something belongs. Notes exist independently until you choose to connect one to a workspace.

Tasks You Can Create Solo

Track your own work directly, no workspace required. Connect a task to a workspace later if it needs to be shared, without losing your personal copy.

Workspaces, When You Need Them

Create a workspace for a project or team, bring in the notes and tasks that belong there, and invite people when the work calls for it.

Search Across Everything

Find anything in seconds, across notes, tasks, and workspaces, or narrowed to exactly one type when you know what you're looking for.

Activity History

Every workspace keeps a chronological record of what changed and when, so you're never digging through chats to reconstruct what happened.

Workspace Discussions

Start a discussion tied to the actual work instead of a separate chat app. Close it once a decision's been made, and it stays part of the workspace history.

Built for Real Workflows

Solo Builders & Indie Hackers

Organize your own notes and tasks in one place instead of across scattered apps and accounts. Turn a project into a workspace later, only if and when it needs to be shared.

Small Teams & Growing Projects

When a project needs more than just you, create a workspace, invite your team, and keep tasks and discussions tied to the actual work instead of scattered across chats and meetings.

Students & Learners

Use workspaces for group projects, or rely on personal notes and tasks for individual study. Either way, everything stays findable.

Developers & Technical Teams

Track issues, document architecture, and maintain clarity across technical work, without adopting a heavyweight project-management tool you'll only use half of.

Under the Hood

Supabase Powered Backend

Authentication, database, and storage, all powered by a modern, scalable backend built for reliability and speed.

Secure by Design

Role based permissions, secure storage, and GDPR aligned data handling ensure your work stays safe and compliant.

Modular Architecture

LogHue is built from isolated modules that scale cleanly. This keeps the interface predictable and prevents features from interfering with each other.

Lightweight, Predictable Interface

LogHue is designed to stay fast and consistent. Every workspace loads cleanly, updates instantly, and keeps your structure intact without unnecessary animations, clutter, or distractions.
