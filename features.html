Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

LogHue keeps your work organized without forcing it into one giant document or scattering it across a dozen apps. Write notes, track tasks, plan your day on a calendar, and connect it all to a workspace only when it actually helps.

Notes

Notes That Stand On Their Own

Write freely without deciding where something belongs. Rich text formatting, custom fonts, text and highlight colors, and autosave. Notes stay independent until you choose to share one with a workspace.

Sketch Notes

Draw and write freely on a canvas with a pen and eraser instead of only typing. Sketch notes are their own note type and export as PNG.

Tables in Notes

Create a table as its own note, or drop one inline into a text note. Export tables to HTML or PDF.

Folders

Group related notes into collapsible folders. Notes outside a folder stay right at the top level, so nothing gets buried.

Export and Share

Export notes as PDF, DOCX, HTML, TXT, or Markdown. Turn on public sharing for a single note to get a link anyone can open, and turn it off again at any time.

ScanHue Text Extraction

Pull text out of an image with OCR, then send it straight into a note to keep editing. Every account gets free monthly credits, and extra credits are available when you need more.

Tasks and Calendar

Tasks You Can Create Solo

Track your own work directly, no workspace required. Mark tasks done, undo it, or duplicate a task into a workspace without losing your personal copy.

Organized by When It's Due

Incomplete tasks are grouped into Overdue, Today, This week, and Later, so the most urgent work is always at the top.

Recurring Tasks

Set a task to repeat daily. Each occurrence is tracked on its own, so finishing today's does not touch tomorrow's.

Deadline Reminders

Get an email 30 minutes and 5 minutes before a deadline, plus optional push notifications. Reminders work for personal and workspace tasks.

Calendar View

See your tasks on a calendar as a second view of the same data. Switch between year, month, week, day, and hour views, with a live line marking the current time. Overdue tasks turn red and finished ones are greyed out.

Workspaces

Workspaces, When You Need Them

Create a workspace for a project or team, bring in the notes and tasks that belong there, and invite people when the work calls for it.

Clear Roles

Owners, admins, and members each have defined permissions. Owners can transfer ownership, admins manage members, and anyone can delete what they created.

Task Logging

Members log progress updates on a task and say when they think it is finished. Only an admin or owner marks it Done, so status always reflects a real decision.

Workspace Discussions

Start named discussion threads tied to the actual work. Messages appear live, replies are nested under the message they answer, and closed threads become read only.

Activity History

Every workspace keeps a chronological record of what changed and when, so you never dig through chats to reconstruct what happened.

API Keys

Generate workspace API keys and read your documents programmatically. Full documentation lives at docs.loghue.com.

Find and Customize

Search Across Everything

Find notes, tasks, and workspaces in seconds. Type "all tasks", "all notes", or "all workspaces" to list everything of one type, or type an item's name to jump straight to it.

Ctrl+K Anywhere

Open the same search from any page with Ctrl+K. Type /notes or any page name to navigate instantly.

Made Yours

Light mode and a custom primary color are built in, and Active Sessions lets you review and sign out your devices from Settings.

Built for Real Workflows

Solo Builders & Indie Hackers

Organize your own notes and tasks in one place instead of across scattered apps and accounts. Turn a project into a workspace later, only if and when it needs to be shared.

Small Teams & Growing Projects

When a project needs more than just you, create a workspace, invite your team, and keep tasks and discussions tied to the actual work instead of scattered across chats and meetings.

Students & Learners

Use workspaces for group projects, or rely on personal notes, sketches, and tasks for individual study. ScanHue turns photos of printed text into notes you can edit.

Developers & Technical Teams

Track issues, document architecture, and connect through the API, without adopting a heavyweight project management tool you will only use half of.

Under the Hood

Supabase Powered Backend

Authentication, database, and realtime updates run on a modern backend built for reliability and speed.

Secure by Design

Role based permissions enforced at the database level, secure session handling, and GDPR aligned data handling keep your work safe.

Lightweight, Predictable Interface

LogHue stays fast and consistent. Every workspace loads cleanly and keeps your structure intact, without clutter or distractions.
